Typed decisions

Fresh measurements on a harder dataset.

Benchmark v2 contains 200 distinct scenarios and 400 scored questions. Each question supplies the facts and rules needed for a short decision. Jev, local Nimble-9B and local Kev-4B were measured through the same gateway, with two samples per question and concurrency 1. v2 has been superseded by benchmark v3: about three quarters of the gap between decision models and thinking models here came from multi-step arithmetic, which v3 precomputes. The v1 rankings remain a separate historical reference.

Dataset v2

200 scenarios. 400 decisions.

Ten equally sized families test exceptions, close boundaries and conflicting evidence. The dataset has 380 multiple-choice questions and 20 open code-extraction questions, with two scored questions per scenario. All necessary rules and facts are supplied.

Two samples per question produce 800 responses per model; repeated samples do not add new scenarios. Questions sharing a scenario are correlated, so confidence intervals describe scored attempts. Read the dataset design →
